About

I am a predoctoral researcher at the Stanford Graduate School of Education, working remotely with Guilherme Lichand.

I hold an MSc in Economics from the London School of Economics and a BSc in Economics from the Universidade de São Paulo. I have research experience in labor and education economics in Brazil. My current interests lie at the intersection of labor economics and macroeconomics.
